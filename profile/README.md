## Palamo Studio

**Governed AI. Disciplined Finance.**

Palamo Studio is a two-person collective — **Hector Luis Alamo** on AI operations and **Rocio Palma** on finance. Bring in either of us on our own, or both together; mix and match to fit what you actually need. Operator-led, based in Henderson, NV.

**[Léelo en español →](README.es.md)**

We help mid-market companies deploy AI — and run the finances around it — with the governance, measurement, and accountability that leadership and regulators demand. We don't just advise: the operators who set the plan are the ones who do the work, with audit trails, rollback design, and CFO-ready reporting built in from day one.

## Ways to Work With Us

Engage one of us, or both — scoped case by case to your goals.

- 🧭 **Hector — AI Operations (Fractional CAIO).** AI leadership without the full-time seat. Owns your AI program end to end: strategy and roadmap, governance/risk/compliance, vendor and model selection, team capability-building, and leadership-ready reporting — and builds and ships the agentic workflows, integrations, and production systems himself.
- 💵 **Rocio — Finance (Fractional CFO).** Finance leadership without the full-time seat, and valuable with or without an AI project in the room: KPI framing and value tracking, process controls and decision gates, NetSuite-driven operations, organizational scaling, and leadership-ready financials from 20+ years in corporate finance. Want automation on top? Her practice can run on agentic tooling from Hector's side — AI-driven insight, built in.
- 🔗 **Both, together.** AI operations and finance as one accountable pairing: every AI dollar tracked, governed, and provably returned, on a shared roadmap and shared reporting. The joint engagement no solo fractional exec can match.
- 🔎 **Assessments.** A short, paid pilot — a low-commitment way to work with either of us before committing to an ongoing seat.
- 📰 **The Crash Log** (crashlog.ai) — a bilingual newsletter on AI and tech gone off the rails, produced by a governed agentic AI system.

We build things that survive production, not just demos.

## How We Work

- **Rigor over speed** — one governed workflow beats three unauditable ones; guardrails and rollback are part of the build, not an afterthought.
- **Honest partnership** — if a simpler approach serves you better, we'll say so.
- **Outcomes over output** — we measure adoption and revenue impact, not lines of code.

Measure before you build; scale only when the numbers say so.
