Three departments:

- AI Engineering: we put AI to work on your workflows, with approval gates, so a person signs off on anything above the limits you set.
- Fractional CFO: finance leadership without a full-time hire.
- AI + Finance: both, as one team, with one roadmap and one report.

Every engagement starts with an assessment: 10 business days, at a fixed fee. $3,000 for AI Engineering, $3,500 for Fractional CFO, $6,000 for AI + Finance. <!-- pending brief: show-pricing --> The AI Engineering assessment shows what AI your team already uses, where it exposes you, which uses are worth doing, ranked by cost and payback, and the rules your leadership signs. <!-- pending brief: what-3000-buys -->

Your assessment fee is credited in full if implementation starts within 90 days. <!-- pending brief: risk-reversal --> At the end of the assessment you get a fixed monthly quote. No obligation to continue. <!-- pending brief: retainer-price -->
